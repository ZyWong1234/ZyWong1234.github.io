About Me

Full-Stack Developer

I'm a computing student who likes building things people actually use. Over the past couple of years I've worked across a real spread of stacks — a production website for a boutique florist on SvelteKit and Cloudflare, coursework platforms in PHP and MySQL, Java, and ASP.NET, and a top-down learning game in Python. I tend to gravitate toward the whole picture of a project: the interface, the logic behind it, and the database underneath.

More recently I've moved into networking and cybersecurity — designing multi-site networks in Cisco Packet Tracer, and running authentication and brute-force testing in Kali Linux. I like understanding not just how to build a system, but how it breaks. Right now I'm looking for a role where I can put that range to work on real problems.
